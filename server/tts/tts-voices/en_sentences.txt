# English TTS test sentences — grouped by evaluation dimension

## short / 短句（节奏、起停）
Hello, how are you today?
That sounds great to me.
I'll be there in five minutes.

## long / 长句（连贯性、换气）
When the morning light spilled across the quiet harbor, the old fisherman untied his boat, checked the nets one last time, and rowed out toward the open water without saying a word.
Although the proposal looked promising on paper, the team agreed that further testing was needed before committing any resources to a full-scale rollout.

## question + exclamation / 疑问与感叹（语调）
Are you sure this is the right address?
What time does the next train leave?
Watch out — that step is slippery!
I can't believe we actually won!

## numbers / dates / 数字与日期（规范化）
The meeting is scheduled for March 3rd, 2026, at 9:45 a.m.
Please call me back at 555-0172 before 6 p.m.
The total comes to $1,284.50, including a 7% service fee.

## proper nouns / 专有名词与缩写（发音）
Dr. Anderson flew from San Francisco to Edinburgh via New York.
The NASA team confirmed the launch with the FAA and the USAF.

## conversational / 口语自然度
Honestly? I had no idea it was going to rain like this.
Well, you know, sometimes things just don't go the way you plan.
Hang on a second, let me grab my coffee.

## phonetic coverage / 音素全覆盖 pangram
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.
How vexingly quick daft zebras jump!

## emotional tone / 情绪表达
I'm so proud of everything you've accomplished this year.
It was a long, lonely winter, and the house felt emptier than ever.
This is, without a doubt, the best news I've heard all week.
